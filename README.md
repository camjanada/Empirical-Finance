# Empirical-Finance
This course focused on the theoretical and empirical analysis of financial asset pricing models and quantitative finance methods. Projects involved developing and testing asset pricing frameworks across multiple asset classes, including equities, fixed income securities, and European options. Coursework emphasized portfolio construction and optimization, risk analysis and hedging strategies, and simulation techniques such as Monte Carlo methods. Quantitative analysis incorporated econometric approaches, including Ordinary Least Squares (OLS), Instrumental Variables (IV), Generalized Method of Moments (GMM), and modeling with non-Gaussian and skewed distributions. Computational work was performed using MATLAB and Excel, with extensive application of vector and matrix-based methods for financial modeling and data analysis.
